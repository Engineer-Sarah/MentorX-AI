### MentorX AI
- Definition：Internal product name for the project — described as 'Pakistan's Personal Career Coach' — a Streamlit-based AI career coaching app built for an AI hackathon.
- Aliases：MentorX

### Career Assessment
- Definition：First step in the core flow: a 20-question Likert-scale quiz covering interests, work style, skills, and values, producing interest scores and derived personality traits fed into downstream AI features.
- Aliases：assessment、quiz

### Career Recommendation
- Definition：AI-generated top-5 career matches tailored to the user's assessment profile, including match scores, reasoning, PKR salary ranges, growth outlook, and key skills — focused on Pakistan's growing economy (tech, freelancing, startups, remote work).
- Aliases：recommendation

### Skill Gap Analysis
- Definition：Comparison of the user's current skills against requirements for a target career, returning matched skills, prioritised gap skills (Critical / Important / Nice-to-have), and an overall match percentage visualised as a Plotly radar chart.
- Aliases：skill gap

### Learning Roadmap
- Definition：A 3-phase, milestone-tracked plan generated to close identified skill gaps, with real resource links, hour estimates, free/paid badges, and a Gantt-style timeline chart.
- Aliases：roadmap

### Resume Analyzer
- Definition：Feature that parses uploaded resume text into sections, scores it (0–100 gauge), provides section-by-section feedback, ATS compatibility tips, and actionable improvement suggestions.
- Aliases：resume review

### AI Mock Interview
- Definition：Chat-based mock interview consisting of 6 questions mixing behavioral, technical, and situational types, with per-answer feedback and a final performance summary.
- Aliases：mock interview

### Career Readiness Dashboard
- Definition：Aggregated view showing a composite readiness score, progress tracker, skill radar chart, score comparison bars, assessment breakdown, and downloadable TXT report.
- Aliases：dashboard

### Pakistani job market
- Definition：Geographic focus of all AI prompts and content — career recommendations, salary ranges (PKR), and learning resources are scoped to opportunities accessible from Pakistan (tech, freelancing, startups, remote work).
- Aliases：PK job market、Pakistan context
